Support

Last updated 29 July 2026

NME Talk has no accounts, so there is no login to recover and no subscription to manage. Most problems fall into one of the few cases below.

“This meeting link is incomplete”

The link lost the part after the #. That fragment holds the encryption key, and it cannot be recovered or regenerated — not by us, not by the server. Ask whoever sent it for the full link.

This usually happens when a link is retyped, wrapped across two lines in an email, or pasted through a tool that strips fragments.

It will not connect on my office or hotel Wi-Fi

Restrictive networks often block the ports video calls normally use. NME Talk detects this and retries through an encrypted relay on port 443, which is the port ordinary web browsing uses. You will see “Connected via relay” when that happens.

If it still fails, the message will say whether a relay was available at all. On a self-hosted deployment, a relay has to be configured for that fallback to exist.

Someone joined who should not have

Anyone holding the link can join — that is how a keyless, accountless meeting works, and it is why the link deserves to be treated as a secret. Start a new meeting to get a new link and key; the old one cannot be un-shared.

For meetings where this matters, turn on the lobby so someone already inside has to admit each arrival.

My camera or microphone will not turn on

Check that the browser or phone has permission, and that no other application is holding the device. On iOS and Android, permissions live under Settings for the NME Talk app.

A meeting still works with either turned off. NME Talk will not connect at all if it cannot encrypt end to end, which is deliberate — joining unencrypted would be worse than not joining.

Can I get a recording or a transcript?

No, and not as a matter of policy — nothing is recorded, so nothing exists to retrieve. The server holds no key capable of decrypting a meeting even if it had kept one.

Reporting abuse

Inside a meeting, press and hold someone's tile on mobile to block them. Blocking is immediate and one-sided: their audio and video stop reaching your device entirely, rather than being hidden behind a placeholder.

The same menu offers block and report. A report carries the room identifier and a timestamp and nothing else, because nothing else exists in readable form anywhere outside the participants' own devices.

Reports are reviewed within one business day. What can be done in response is real but bounded: a room can be revoked and a network address blocked. What cannot be done is review the content of the meeting, since it is encrypted end to end and no operator holds the key. We would rather say that plainly than imply an investigation that is not possible.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services rather than us.

Reporting a security issue

Security reports are welcome and taken seriously. Please email support@nmetalk.com rather than opening a public issue, and allow reasonable time to fix before disclosure. The whole system is open source, so you are also welcome to read it first.
